# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Receive architectural guidance and design reviews; collaborate on technical approach
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Work with QA to understand test requirements and validate acceptance criteria
- **Product Managers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria; provide technical input on feasibility
- **Project Managers**: Report progress, blockers, and risks in daily standups and planning sessions
- **UX/Design Lead**: Collaborate on implementation of design specifications and user experience requirements

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Align on timelines, milestones, and resource needs; collaborate on release planning
- **Developers**: Define acceptance criteria and features; review implementation against requirements
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define quality standards and success metrics; validate test coverage
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Provide business context and gather approval on prioritization
- **UX/Design Lead**: Collaborate on user research, design specifications, and usability metrics

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on roadmap, priorities, and resource allocation
- **Developers**: Track progress, manage dependencies, and escalate blockers
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Coordinate on technical dependencies and risk mitigation
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate test phases and release readiness
- **Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator**: Collaborate on process execution and team health
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Provide regular status updates and escalate critical decisions

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They work with developers and product teams to validate that solutions meet acceptance criteria, perform well, and are ready for production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality standards aligned with acceptance criteria
- Build and maintain automated test suites (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Lead manual testing for exploratory and UAT scenarios
- Triage bugs and coordinate with developers for resolution
- Validate pre-release requirements and conduct smoke tests
- Track test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure quality and user confidence in releases
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, reliable feedback loops for developers

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Daily standups and blockers escalation
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Release readiness sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test requirements, bug triage, and resolution; provide fast feedback loops
- **Product Managers**: Align on quality standards, acceptance criteria, and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Report testing status, blockers, and release readiness in planning and status meetings
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Coordinate testing strategy for complex technical components
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Participate in UAT and provide release readiness certification

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and architectural oversight. They work with developers to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architectural decisions
- Review design and code for technical soundness
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor developers and support technical problem-solving
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Collaborate on dependency management and integration points

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable team learning and growth

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical spike discussions
- Code reviews and mentoring
- Risk register contributions
- Architecture documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, conduct code reviews, and mentor on best practices
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks, dependencies, and resource needs; coordinate with other teams
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test strategy for technical components and performance requirements
- **Product Managers**: Provide technical feasibility input for feature prioritization and trade-off decisions
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Communicate technical strategy and risk mitigation plans

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, executive alignment, and governance. They represent business interests and ensure projects deliver value aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and key milestones
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Remove organizational blockers
- Represent business interests in trade-off decisions
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and communication
- Participate in gate reviews and go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Ensure business value delivery
- Maintain stakeholder alignment
- Reduce organizational friction and delays

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and initiation alignment
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone gate reviews
- Executive escalation for critical decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive regular status updates and provide executive oversight; approve scope and priority changes
- **Product Managers**: Align on business goals, priorities, and resource allocation
- **Developers**: Participate in UAT, provide business context, and attend key demos
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Approve release readiness and participate in UAT
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Review technical strategy and risk mitigation for business impact

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Delivery Facilitators remove blockers, facilitate ceremonies, and ensure team processes run smoothly. They support the team's ability to deliver efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, retrospectives, and reviews
- Remove blockers and escalate impediments
- Ensure team processes and workflows are followed
- Track and report on team health and velocity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Coach the team on agile practices

### Goals
- Enable smooth, uninterrupted delivery
- Build high-performing teams
- Continuously improve processes and team effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Blocker escalation and resolution
- Team health and velocity reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **All team members**: Facilitate ceremonies, remove blockers, and foster psychological safety
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on process improvement, report team health and velocity metrics
- **Developers**: Remove impediments, coach on agile practices, facilitate technical discussions
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and prioritization discussions

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads define user experience, design systems, and usability standards. They work with product and development teams to ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with design principles.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience and design approach
- Create design systems and component libraries
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Review implementations for design fidelity and accessibility
- Document design decisions and rationale
- Collaborate on user acceptance and satisfaction

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design consistency and brand alignment
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Usability testing and research findings
- Design system documentation
- User feedback and iteration planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design specifications and conduct implementation reviews; collaborate on design system components
- **Product Managers**: Conduct user research, validate design decisions, and measure user satisfaction
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design phases and communicate timeline impact of design changes
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define usability test scenarios and evaluate user experience quality
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Present design decisions, conduct user testing feedback, and drive user satisfaction metrics
